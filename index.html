Welcome to My Website

Hi! My name is Kayla, and I’m a combined Computer Science and Business Administration student at Northeastern University with interests in data analysis, product development, and technology-driven solutions. I am a proactive, collaborative, and detail-oriented team member with strong problem-solving, communication, and creative skills. I enjoy applying both technical and strategic thinking to projects and am motivated to make a meaningful impact through my work.

I've worked as a Product Consultant for Sidewalk Entertainment in Seoul, South Korea, where I contributed to STEPIN, an AI-powered dance app. Working on a product with a team in another country was a great way to see how an idea moves from concept to something people actually use. I also completed a co-op on the Web Store Operations team at TJX Companies, which gave me a close look at how e-commerce runs day to day at a large retailer.

On campus, I'm active in Barkada, Northeastern's Filipino cultural club, where I've served as Historian and Vice President. Helping run a club has been a practical lesson in leading a team and keeping a community's traditions alive. In my free time, I like editing photos and video, doing nails, and exploring new restaurants and cafes.

This site collects the projects I care about. The Exploration page covers a data visualization project on health across Boston neighborhoods, built with CDC PLACES data. Take a look around, and feel free to reach out.
